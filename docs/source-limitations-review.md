## Review disposition

Labs 14–19 were reviewed. They add methodological and lab-reported checking evidence but no original October raw response or project-specific Yahoo permission. The assignment requires sources, as-of boundaries, reproducible evidence and handling licensing; it does not list a separate permission letter, October receipt, or replacement dataset as a standalone deliverable. These open findings remain rubric considerations, not newly invented submission items.

The documentary review is complete. Original October-vintage authentication and Yahoo rights are **not resolved**. The decision memo, README, validation report, and rubric map carry those limitations; no full-credit or permission claim follows from their disclosure.

The later Yahoo capture and older DGS10 capture have different retrieval boundaries. They form a classroom demonstration using a frozen price sample and dated monitoring snapshot, not a single historical backtest. DGS10 is not an optimizer input, and its correction does not change portfolio arithmetic.

No price replacement or new protected experiment is implied by this review. A licensed-source replacement would require its own provenance, numerical comparison, updated results/documents, and fresh recordings if results change. The preserved student prediction and execution evidence must remain intact.
